    Waiver rules

    Overdue fees only. A waiver covers the overdue fee, not principal, interest or other dues and charges.
    Match the DPD stage. Use the bracket for the customer's current stage. The cap is 75% at DPD 90+.
    Authorization. Process the waiver under the applicable authorization and system process.
    Credit bureau reporting. From 1 October 2026, accounts with a waiver are reported as “Closed”, not “Settled”.
    Internal tool. Check every waiver against current policy before you apply it.
